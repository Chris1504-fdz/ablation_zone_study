# Ablation Zone Study — Composite Formulation Analysis & Optimization

Statistical analysis and multi-objective Bayesian optimization (MOBO) of an ablative
composite material (carbon black + Fe-rich regolith filler), a UIC–Northwestern
collaboration (MIRO project). NU builds the models and recommends formulations;
UIC fabricates and tests them.

## Problem Setup

| | Variable | Range / Unit |
|---|---|---|
| **Input X1** | Carbon black content | 5 – 20 wt% |
| **Input X2** | Fe-rich regolith content | 1 – 5 wt% |
| **Output Y1** | Linear ablative rate (minimize) | µm/s |
| **Output Y2** | Backside temperature (minimize) | °C |
| **Output Y3** | Density (minimize) | g/cm³ |

The three objectives conflict (more carbon black → less ablation and lower backside
temperature, but higher density), so the goal is to map the Pareto front with as few
experiments as possible. Each fabricated sample also contains I-369 at 2 wt%; resin
fills the remainder.

## Data

`data/ablation_data.xlsx` — **45 measurements: 15 conditions × 3 replicates** =
the initial 13-condition DOE plus the two BO campaign samples (19.6 / 2.0, July 2026;
17.4 / 1.0, August 2026). The MOBO notebooks train on per-condition means with the
replicate scatter as observation noise; the analysis notebook uses all replicates.

**To add a new iteration:** save UIC's results workbook under `data/`, append one row
per replicate to `ablation_data.xlsx`, add the condition to `ITERATION_SAMPLES` in
`mobo_analysis_v4.ipynb` Section 0, and follow the "pending" template at the end of the
last iteration section. The notebooks hold campaign rows out of the "initial DOE", so
historical sections reproduce exactly after re-runs.

## Notebooks

- **`mobo_analysis_v4.ipynb` — active campaign notebook.** One section per real
  iteration, each running the same five methods of the `Campaign` class from
  `notebooks/utils/`: `propose` (fit on data through the previous iteration, maximize
  qLogEHVI, record the sample *requested* from UIC and the predictions sent with it;
  historical requests are *pinned* to what was actually sent), `import_results` (the
  replicates *provided* by UIC, read from their workbook or typed from the email, and
  cross-checked against the spreadsheet), `compare` (received vs. provided: coverage of
  the sent interval and of the model's 95 % prediction interval, standardized error of
  the condition mean), `update` (Pareto front and hypervolume before/after), and
  `improvement` (best-so-far per output, hypervolume growth, Pareto composition, utopia
  gap, what each sample added). Before a new request, `stopping_check` applies the
  **probabilistic regret bound** of Wilson (NeurIPS 2024) in a hypervolume form (Section 6,
  step by step: `prb_setup` → `prb_draw` → `prb_regret` → `prb_decide` → `prb_objectives`
  → `prb_conclusion`; `utils/stopping.py`): stop when, with probability ≥ 1 − δ under the model, no single
  further formulation would improve the hypervolume by more than ε (exact joint posterior
  draws on a grid, one-step hypervolume improvement via box decomposition, sequential
  Clopper–Pearson test with the paper's schedules, δ spread over the remaining budget); the
  whole-front coverage and the paper's single-output rule per objective are shown as
  complements, Section 6.7 (`stopping_trajectory`) traces the rule over the campaign, and
  Section 6.8 renders the one plain-language figure to share (`stopping_summary_figure`).
  Section 7 holds the iteration-3 proposal; Section 8 summarizes
  the whole campaign (8.1: requested vs. provided table and figure — `performance_table` /
  `performance_figure`, the sent prediction and range against UIC's replicates; prediction
  accuracy; improvement dashboard; GP response surfaces) and Section 9 exports
  `outputs/v4/` including the `share/` files. Model: fixed-noise `SingleTaskGP` per objective
  (`train_Yvar = s²/n`), qLogEHVI with a **seeded** optimizer (a proposal depends only on
  the data, not on which cells ran before). Runs on the `ml_gp_env` kernel.

- **`mobo_analysis_v3.ipynb` — previous active version, now a cross-check.** Same
  model as v4 in the original linear layout (Sections 1–9 initial-data analysis and a
  simulated loop, Section 10 campaign log, Section 12 iteration-gain analysis).
  Re-run Sep 10, 2026 with iteration 2 recorded; its iteration-3 proposal is identical
  to v4's (same seed).

- **`mobo_analysis_v2.ipynb` — iteration-1 record.** Homoscedastic GPs; reproduces v1
  and the numbers sent to UIC in July 2026. Its own iteration-2 proposal (17.3 / 1.1)
  was not fabricated (the v3 proposal was); Section 10 now also evaluates the v2 model
  at the sent location against the Aug 26 results as a cross-model check (its
  latent-only CI again misses 2 of 3 Y2 replicates, which the v3/v4 prediction interval
  covers).

- **`mobo_analysis.ipynb` (v1) — frozen reference** that produced the Jul 10, 2026
  recommendation (X1 = 19.579, X2 = 1.986, qLogEHVI = 0.785).

- **`ablation_analysis.ipynb` — "What does the data tell us?"** Design orthogonality
  checks, two-way ANOVA (additive main effects + additive-vs-full interaction F-test,
  because the augmented design is an incomplete factorial: 15 of 35 grid cells),
  quadratic regression per output, and a heteroscedastic multi-output GP (GPyTorch, ICM
  kernel). Re-run on the 45-row dataset; response-surface figures mark both tested
  campaign samples and the iteration-3 proposal (`CAMPAIGN_SAMPLES` / `PROPOSED_NEXT`
  in Section 1 — keep in sync with v4).

**Two real BO iterations are complete** (sources: `resources/*.pdf`).

| Iteration | Requested (sent) | Fabricated | Results from UIC | Predicted (sent) vs. observed mean |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 19.58 / 1.99 — Jul 10, 2026 (v1, EHVI 0.785) | 19.6 / 2.0 | Jul 20, 2026: Y1 5.65 / 5.68 / 6.27, Y2 187 / 178 / 182, Y3 1.216 ×3 | Y1 5.968 → 5.867; Y2 177.7 → 182.3 (2 of 3 outside the sent latent CI); Y3 1.216 → 1.216 |
| 2 | 17.352 / 1.000 — Aug 12, 2026 (v3, qLogEHVI 0.531) | 17.4 / 1.0 | Aug 26, 2026: Y1 5.61 / 6.02 / 6.05, Y2 189 / 202 / 195, Y3 1.194 ×3 | Y1 5.62 → 5.893; Y2 194.2 → 195.3; Y3 1.1943 → 1.194 — **all 9 replicate values inside the sent 95 % ranges**, standardized errors ≤ 0.28 σ |

Effect on the objective space (hypervolume on condition means, reference point fixed
from the initial DOE): **51.168 → 51.974 → 52.503 (+2.61 %)**; with replicates as
points (email convention) 51.168 → 53.240 → 54.994. Pareto front 12 → 13 → 14
solutions; both BO samples are non-dominated and the iteration-2 sample dominates the
DOE condition (12.5, 5). Best single-output values so far: Y1 5.867 µm/s (iteration 1,
−3.2 % vs. the DOE best); Y2 and Y3 bests are still DOE conditions (168.3 °C at 20/5,
1.135 g/cm³ at 5/1) — the campaign is filling the low-ablation part of the front
rather than pushing the extremes.

**Iteration-3 proposal** (v4 Section 7, `outputs/v4/iteration3_recommended_sample.xlsx`):
**X1 = 20.000, X2 = 2.156 → carbon black 20.0 wt%, Fe-rich regolith 2.2 wt%** (I-369
2 wt%, resin 75.8 wt%), qLogEHVI −0.441. Predicted: Y1 5.92 µm/s (PI 4.6 – 7.2),
Y2 178.4 °C (PI 157.5 – 199.4), Y3 1.2195 g/cm³ (PI 1.2172 – 1.2218). Note that the
point sits on the carbon-black upper bound next to the iteration-1 sample, and the
acquisition value has fallen each round (0.785 → 0.531 → −0.441 on the log scale): the
optimizer is now exploiting the low-ablation / low-temperature corner and expects
smaller gains inside the current bounds. Whether to test it, widen the X1 range, or add
a density constraint is a project decision to make before sending it.

**Should we stop?** (v4 Section 6, probabilistic regret bound, δ = 0.05, planned budget
10 iterations, 2000 posterior draws; regret = largest hypervolume gain one more formulation
could give under a posterior draw):

| ε (share of current HV) | P̂(regret ≤ ε) | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 1 % (0.53 HV units) | 0.09 | continue |
| 2 % (1.05) | 0.70 | continue |
| 5 % (2.63) | 0.99 | stop |
| 10 % (5.25) | 1.00 | stop |

The expected best one-step gain has fallen 3.49 → 2.57 → 0.94 HV units over the three
checks (before iterations 1, 2, 3; v4 Section 6.7), and the tested set holds ≈ 92 % of the
hypervolume the model believes attainable (84 % → 89 % → 92 %). Per output, the paper's single-objective
rule says the Y2 and Y3 optima are already found (within 5 % of the DOE range with
probability ≥ 0.975) while the best Y1 is not yet (P ≈ 0.75). So: keep going if a gain of
1–2 % of hypervolume per experiment is still worth a fabrication; stop if 5 % is the
resolution that matters. As the paper stresses, the verdict is model-based — it uses the
same replicate-noise GPs as the proposals.

## Dependencies
